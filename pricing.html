Billing questions

Good to know.

Do I need a card for the trial?
No. The trial starts when you finish setting up your account and ends on its own after 14 days. Nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.
What happens when the trial or my plan ends?
You go back to Free. Every session stays in your account. Pro sections like Scores and Debate are hidden until you upgrade again, and you can open new sessions as long as you are within the Free limit.
Pro monthly, yearly or a Conference Pass?
Chairing every month or two? Yearly is the best value. Chairing just one conference? The pass gives you Pro for a full week with one payment and nothing to cancel.
Who handles the payment?
Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. They take the payment, work out VAT for your country and send the invoice. Gavel never sees your card details.
Can my school or MUN club pay?
Yes. For a whole conference, ask about the Secretariat licence: Pro for every chair, your branding in every room and one invoice. Write to us.
Can I get a refund?
If you are in the EU you can withdraw within 14 days of your first purchase. Otherwise, write to us within 14 days and we will sort it out. See the terms.
