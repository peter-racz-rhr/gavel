FAQ

Questions chairs ask.

Is Gavel free?
Yes — Gavel is free while it is in beta. If paid plans come later, you will hear about it well in advance, and your existing sessions stay yours.
Does it follow real MUN rules of procedure?
Gavel follows the usual flow of most conferences: roll call with quorum at one-third, opening speeches, the general speakers list with yields, moderated and unmoderated caucuses, motions ranked by disruptiveness, and recorded votes with simple or two-thirds majorities. You stay in control of every decision.
Do I need internet during the session?
Gavel keeps working offline and saves to the device. When the connection comes back, your sessions sync to your account.
How does the projector work?
Click Present, then open Gavel in a second window on the projector and choose “Make this the projector”. It follows your main window live, so you run everything from your laptop.
What do you do with my data?
We store your name, email and the sessions you create, only to run Gavel for you. We never sell your data. Read the privacy policy for the details.
